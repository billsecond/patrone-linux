FONTLOG for the PatroneOS fonts
===============================

This file gives details about PatroneOS's font families: what they are,
how they're made, and their history. It is distributed with the fonts, as
the SIL Open Font License suggests.


Basic font information
----------------------

The PatroneOS fonts are five families, made for PatroneOS and free for
anyone to use, change and share under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1 (OFL.txt).

- PatroneOS Sans, the system font: Light, Regular, SemiBold, Bold and
  ExtraBold, each with an italic.
- PatroneOS Mono, the console font: every glyph one cell wide, a dotted
  zero, and the box-drawing and block characters terminal programs draw
  their frames and bars with. Light, Regular and Bold, each with an italic.
- PatroneOS Document, for documents: Regular and Bold, each with an italic.
- PatroneOS Serif, for serif text: Regular and Bold, each with an italic.
- PatroneOS Classic, a plain, classic sans: Regular and Bold, each with an
  italic.

Every family has a tailed l, so that I, l, 1 and | all differ, and no zero
is slashed. Each covers Latin-1, Latin Extended-A, the common Western
punctuation and symbols, Romanian's comma letters, arrows and a few maths
signs. The italics are the uprights slanted.

The fonts are static TrueType, hinted with ttfautohint, with WOFF2 copies
for the web.


How they're made
----------------

The fonts are drawn from scratch, in code. Every letter is a pen stroke:
a centre line of straight lines and cubic curves, outlined by an oval pen
that turns and changes size along it (source/engine.py). The letters'
skeletons, the pen and each family's character are in source/; build.sh
draws the families, writes them as UFO sources, compiles them with fontmake,
hints them with ttfautohint and checks them with fontbakery.

No outline, point, kerning pair or hint was taken from any other font, and
nothing was traced. Most of the families' advance widths and line spacing
follow widely used fonts (source/metrics/), so that text set for those
fonts keeps its layout.

The fonts were made with AI help: the drawing code was written with an AI
assistant, under William Daugherty's direction, and every family was
checked and approved by him.


ChangeLog
---------

30 October 2026 (William Daugherty) PatroneOS fonts version 1.1.0
- PatroneOS Sans redrawn: new letter shapes, proportions and spacing,
  narrower letters, a single-storey g, and a heavier Bold. SemiBold is now
  drawn in its own right.
- PatroneOS Mono, Document, Serif and Classic have the same glyphs as in
  1.0.0.

29 October 2026 (William Daugherty) PatroneOS fonts version 1.0.0
- First release: PatroneOS Sans, Mono, Document, Serif and Classic, 28
  fonts in all.
