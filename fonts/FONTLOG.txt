FONTLOG for Patrone Sans
========================

This file records how Patrone Sans was made and how it changes, as the SIL
Open Font License's FAQ suggests.


Basic font information
----------------------

Patrone Sans is the interface typeface of PatroneOS: a humanist sans for
screens, with open apertures, a large x-height and a little contrast. It
comes as one variable font with one axis, weight, from 300 to 900, and six
named weights: Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold and Black.

    PatroneSans[wght].ttf

It covers Basic Latin, Latin-1 and Latin Extended-A, Romanian's letters
with a comma below, and the punctuation and signs the desktop uses: 404
glyphs. Greek, Cyrillic and other scripts come from Noto Sans, which the
system's font settings put after it.

License: SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL.txt), with no Reserved
Font Name.


How it was made, and with AI help
---------------------------------

Patrone Sans 2 was drawn from scratch with AI help: an AI coding
assistant wrote the drawing code in source/ to William Daugherty's brief
(a humanist sans like the interface fonts PatroneOS has used, six
weights, a plain zero, I, l and 1 easy to tell apart, Patrone Sans 1's
line spacing), and he directed the design and accepted it, as with
PatroneOS's logo (docs/COMPLIANCE.md, item 9). Material an AI generates
without enough human authorship may not be protected by copyright, so the
font is offered under the OFL only as far as anyone holds copyright in it.

No outline, point, measurement or metric was traced, copied, converted,
interpolated or taken from Noto Sans, Segoe UI or any other font, and no
image model was used. Every letter is a skeleton (straight lines and
curves through points chosen for that letter, written as code) drawn with
an oval pen, wider than it is tall, whose size each point can scale; the
engine (source/engine.py) outlines each stroke, fitting both of its sides
with curves, and the variable font keeps the strokes overlapping where they
meet. The numbers the letters are drawn with (stem weights, x-height,
counters, sidebearings) were chosen for this font. The one thing kept from
Patrone Sans 1 is its line spacing (ascender 1069, descender 293, no line
gap), so that switching fonts moves nothing on screen.

The shapes avoid the distinctive letters of other interface fonts, Segoe
UI's above all: its name is Microsoft's trademark, and some countries
protect typeface designs.


ChangeLog
---------

2026-10-29 (William Daugherty) Patrone Sans 2.000

- A new typeface, drawn from scratch (see above), in place of Patrone
  Sans 1, which was Noto Sans 2.015 with a tailed l and a slashed zero.
  Nothing of Noto Sans is left in it.

- Six weights in one variable font: Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold,
  Bold and Black (300 to 900). Patrone Sans 1's thin and extra light
  weights and its italic are gone: apps that ask for an italic get a
  slanted Patrone Sans from fontconfig.

- A plain zero, told from O by its shape: a narrow oval, where O is a wide
  round, about half as wide again. Patrone Sans 1's zero had a slash.

- I, l, 1 and | are four different shapes: l has a tail, I is a plain
  stroke shorter than l, 1 has a flag and a foot, and | runs from below the
  baseline to above the capitals. The foot also fills the tabular 1's
  width, so "10" doesn't gape.

- Latin only: Basic Latin, Latin-1, Latin Extended-A, Ș ș Ț ț, and the
  desktop's punctuation and signs. Greek and Cyrillic, which Patrone Sans 1
  had from Noto Sans, now come from Noto Sans itself.

- Tabular figures by default, as before; no proportional or oldstyle
  figures, superscripts beyond ¹ ² ³, or other OpenType features beyond
  kerning, accents (mark and mkmk), capitals' flatter accents and dotless
  i and j under accents (ccmp).

- No hinting, as before, and a gasp table asking for smoothing at every
  size; a prep program sets dropout control only, for rasterizers that run
  TrueType programs. GNOME's default hinting ("slight") hints every
  TrueType font with FreeType's autohinter.

2026-10-26 (William Daugherty) Patrone Sans 1.000

- Noto Sans 2.015 with a tailed l and a slashed zero.
